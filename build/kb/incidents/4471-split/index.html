The three problems

parser: the shared parser reads dates month first. Every tenant on file writes the day first, so a booking for 7 April lands on 4 July. This is the bug #4471 names. It reaches people through the app, email and the booking-v2 form.

hint: the booking-v3 form asks for the wrong order. It prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. People type what it asks for, month first, and the form reads it the tenant’s way, day first. Only booking-v3 tenants, and only through the form. The parser fix does not touch it: the form hint has to change.

shift: dates move by one day. No day and month swap moves a date by one day. These reports say “Tuesday showed as Monday”, and one client in Lisbon and one in Toronto saw different days for the same meeting. That points at time zones, not date order. The desk noted these reports (desk-notes.json) but counted them under parser.

How they got merged

Decision 0001 merges reports whose subject lines match. People describe all three problems in the same words (“Date is wrong”, “Wrong day on the invite”), so the subject cannot tell them apart. Intake’s tag cannot either: it guesses from the reporter’s browser, and the table counts how often that guess names another problem. What tells them apart is the tenant (its locale and booking form), the channel, and what the report says happened to the date.

What to do

  • Keep #4471 for the parser fix only, and link the other two tickets from it.
  • Fix the booking-v3 date hint to match the tenant’s locale.
  • Find where the one-day shift comes from before more reports get filed as parser.
  • Write a new decision record to replace 0001 for date reports: merge by cause, not by subject. Decision 0001 stays as it is; a new record replaces it.
